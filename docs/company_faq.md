# Acme Cloud FAQ

## Pricing
Acme Cloud has three plans. The Starter plan is free and includes 5 GB of storage.
The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month and includes 1 TB of storage and priority support.
The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, SSO, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Refunds
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
Monthly subscriptions are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST, by email at support@acme.example.
Pro and Enterprise customers also get live chat. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support.

# Horizon Trust Bank: Customer Handbook

*Horizon Trust Bank is a fictional bank. This handbook is sample data for testing a chatbot.*

## 1. About the Bank

Horizon Trust Bank was founded in 1998 and has its head office in Pune, Maharashtra. It runs 340 branches and more than 1,200 ATMs across India. The bank offers savings and current accounts, fixed and recurring deposits, loans, credit and debit cards, and digital banking through the Horizon Mobile app and Horizon NetBanking.

Customer care number: 1800-200-4455 (toll free, 24x7)

## 2. Branch and Service Hours

- Branches are open Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## 3. Savings Accounts

### 3.1 Types of savings accounts

| Account | Minimum Average Balance (Metro/Urban) | Minimum Average Balance (Semi-urban/Rural) | Interest Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Savings (BSBDA) | Nil | Nil | 2.75% p.a. |
| Classic Savings | ₹10,000 | ₹5,000 | 3.00% p.a. |
| Premium Savings | ₹25,000 | ₹15,000 | 3.25% p.a. |
| Elite Savings | ₹1,00,000 | ₹1,00,000 | 3.50% p.a. up to ₹10 lakh; 4.00% p.a. above ₹10 lakh |
| Junior Savings (age under 18) | ₹2,500 | ₹1,000 | 3.00% p.a. |
| Senior Citizen Savings (age 60+) | ₹5,000 | ₹2,500 | 3.50% p.a. |

Savings account interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited to the account every quarter, in March, June, September and December.

### 3.2 Charges for not maintaining the minimum balance

If the monthly average balance falls below the required minimum, a charge applies:

- Shortfall up to 25%: ₹100 + GST
- Shortfall of 25% to 50%: ₹200 + GST
- Shortfall above 50%: ₹400 + GST

Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA) never have a minimum balance charge. The bank sends an SMS and email alert when the balance falls below the minimum, and the customer has one month to restore it before any charge is applied.

### 3.3 Opening a savings account

To open a savings account, a customer needs:

1. A recent passport-size photograph.
2. PAN card, or Form 60 if the customer does not have a PAN.
3. Proof of identity and address. Accepted documents are Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID card, or driving licence.
4. An initial deposit equal to the minimum balance for the chosen account (not needed for Basic Savings).

Accounts can be opened at any branch or fully online through Video KYC in the Horizon Mobile app. Video KYC is available from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, and takes about 10 minutes. The customer must be physically present in India and have their original PAN card and Aadhaar-linked mobile number ready.

### 3.4 Closing a savings account

- Accounts closed within 14 days of opening: no charge.
- Accounts closed after 14 days but within 12 months: ₹500 + GST.
- Accounts closed after 12 months: no charge.

To close an account, the customer must submit the account closure form at their home branch, return unused cheque leaves and the debit card, and transfer or withdraw the remaining balance.

## 5. Fixed Deposits (FD)

### 5.1 FD interest rates (deposits below ₹3 crore)

| Tenure | General Public | Senior Citizens (60+) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 days to 45 days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46 days to 179 days | 4.75% | 5.25% |
| 180 days to 364 days | 6.00% | 6.50% |
| 1 year to less than 2 years | 6.90% | 7.40% |
| 2 years to less than 3 years | 7.10% | 7.60% |
| 3 years to 5 years | 7.00% | 7.50% |
| 5 years to 10 years | 6.75% | 7.25% |
| Tax Saver FD (5-year lock-in) | 7.00% | 7.50% |

Super senior citizens (age 80 and above) receive an additional 0.10% over the senior citizen rate.

The minimum FD amount is ₹5,000. Interest can be paid out monthly, quarterly, or at maturity (cumulative). Deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor.

### 5.2 Premature withdrawal

- FDs can be closed before maturity, except Tax Saver FDs, which cannot be withdrawn during the 5-year lock-in period.
- A penalty of 1% is deducted from the interest rate applicable for the period the deposit actually stayed with the bank.
- No penalty applies if the FD is closed within 7 days of opening, but no interest is paid either.
- Senior citizens pay a reduced penalty of 0.50%.

### 5.3 Tax on FD interest

TDS (tax deducted at source) is deducted at 10% if FD interest exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year (₹50,000 for senior citizens). If PAN is not provided, TDS is deducted at 20%. Customers whose total income is below the taxable limit can submit Form 15G (or Form 15H for senior citizens) at the start of each financial year to avoid TDS.

### 5.4 Auto-renewal

Unless the customer gives different instructions, FDs are automatically renewed on maturity for the same tenure at the interest rate applicable on the renewal date.

## 6. Recurring Deposits (RD)

- Minimum monthly instalment: ₹500, in multiples of ₹100.
- Tenure: 6 months to 10 years, in multiples of 3 months.
- Interest rates are the same as FD rates for the matching tenure.
- A missed instalment attracts a penalty of ₹1.50 per ₹100 per month of delay.
- If six consecutive instalments are missed, the RD is closed and the balance with applicable interest is paid to the customer's savings account.

## 7. Loans

### 7.1 Home Loan

- Interest rate: 8.50% to 9.65% p.a. (floating), linked to the bank's Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR). The exact rate depends on the applicant's credit score.
  - Credit score 800 and above: 8.50%
  - Credit score 750 to 799: 8.75%
  - Credit score 700 to 749: 9.15%
  - Credit score below 700: 9.65%
- Women borrowers (as primary applicant) get a 0.05% discount.
- Maximum tenure: 30 years, or until the borrower turns 70, whichever is earlier.
- Loan amount: up to 90% of the property value for loans up to ₹30 lakh; 80% for loans between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh; 75% for loans above ₹75 lakh.
- Processing fee: 0.50% of the loan amount, with a maximum of ₹15,000 + GST.
- Prepayment and foreclosure: no charges on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers.

### 7.2 Personal Loan

- Interest rate: 10.75% to 18.00% p.a. (fixed), depending on credit score, income and employer category.
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹40 lakh.
- Tenure: 12 to 72 months.
- Processing fee: up to 2% of the loan amount + GST.
- Eligibility: salaried individuals aged 21 to 60 with a minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000 and at least 1 year in their current job; self-employed individuals aged 25 to 65 with at least 2 years of business continuity.
- Foreclosure: allowed after 12 EMIs have been paid. A charge of 4% of the outstanding principal applies if foreclosed between 13 and 24 months; 2% after 24 months.
- Pre-approved personal loans for existing customers are disbursed instantly through the mobile app.

### 7.3 Car Loan

- Interest rate: 8.90% to 11.50% p.a.
- Up to 100% on-road funding for selected models; otherwise up to 90% of the on-road price.
- Tenure: up to 7 years for new cars, up to 5 years for used cars (the car must be under 8 years old at the end of the loan tenure).
- Processing fee: ₹5,000 + GST flat.

### 7.4 Education Loan

- Interest rate: 9.25% to 11.25% p.a. Female students get a 0.50% discount.
- Up to ₹7.5 lakh: no collateral required.
- Above ₹7.5 lakh: collateral security is required.
- Repayment starts 12 months after the course is completed, or 6 months after getting a job, whichever is earlier (the moratorium period).
- Repayment tenure: up to 15 years after the moratorium.
- Interest paid on education loans is eligible for a tax deduction under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act.

### 7.5 Gold Loan

- Interest rate: 9.00% to 10.50% p.a.
- Loan amount: up to 75% of the market value of the gold ornaments.
- Tenure: 3 to 12 months. Bullet repayment (pay all principal and interest at the end) is allowed.
- Only gold ornaments of 18 to 22 carat purity are accepted. Gold bars and coins above 50 grams are not accepted.

### 7.6 Missed EMI payments

- A late payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount applies.
- A cheque or ECS/NACH bounce charge of ₹500 + GST applies for each bounced payment.
- Missed payments are reported to credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark) and will lower the borrower's credit score.
- Customers facing financial difficulty should contact their branch before missing a payment. The bank may offer EMI restructuring in genuine cases such as job loss or medical emergency.

## 11. Fraud, Security and Lost Cards

### 11.1 What the bank will never ask for

Horizon Trust Bank will never ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV, full card number, NetBanking password or UPI PIN over phone, SMS, email or social media. Bank staff will never ask you to install screen-sharing apps such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer. Any such request is a fraud attempt.

### 11.2 Reporting fraud or a lost or stolen card

1. Block the card immediately through the mobile app (Cards > Block Card), by calling 1800-200-4455, or by sending an SMS "BLOCK <last 4 digits of card>" to 56767.
2. Report unauthorised transactions to the bank as soon as possible.
3. Also report cyber fraud to the national cyber crime helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

### 11.3 Customer liability for unauthorised electronic transactions

As per RBI guidelines:

- **Zero liability:** if the fraud happened because of the bank's negligence, or due to a third-party breach where neither the bank nor the customer is at fault, and the customer reports it within 3 working days.
- **Limited liability:** if the third-party breach is reported within 4 to 7 working days, the customer's maximum liability is ₹5,000 to ₹25,000, depending on the account type.
- **Full liability:** if the loss happened because the customer shared payment credentials (such as OTP or PIN), the customer bears the entire loss until they report it to the bank. Any loss after reporting is borne by the bank.
- **Beyond 7 working days:** liability is decided according to the bank's board-approved policy.

The bank will credit the disputed amount to the customer's account (shadow reversal) within 10 working days of the complaint, and will resolve the complaint within 90 days.

## 12. KYC Updates

- KYC must be updated periodically: every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.
- If there is no change in KYC details, customers can confirm this through the app, NetBanking, email, or by visiting a branch. No fresh documents are needed.
- If KYC is not updated after reminders, the account may be partially frozen. Credits will be allowed but debits will be blocked until KYC is updated.

## 13. Nomination and Deceased Customers

- Customers can add up to 4 nominees for deposit accounts and specify the share for each.
- Nomination can be added or changed at any time through the app, NetBanking, or at a branch using Form DA-1 (add) or DA-3 (change).
- When an account holder dies, the nominee must submit the death certificate, their own KYC documents, and the claim form. Claims with a nominee are settled within 15 days of receiving all documents.
- If there is no nominee, legal heirs must provide a legal heir certificate or succession certificate, depending on the claim amount.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does it take to get a debit card after opening an account?**
A: A personalised debit card is delivered to the registered address within 7 working days. A virtual debit card is available instantly in the mobile app.

**Q: Can I link my account to more than one UPI app?**
A: Yes. You can link your Horizon Trust account to any UPI app, such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM.

**Q: What happens if I deposit money into the wrong account number?**
A: Contact your branch or customer care immediately with the transaction details. The bank will contact the beneficiary's bank, but the money can only be returned with the beneficiary's consent. The bank cannot reverse the transaction on its own.

**Q: How do I update my mobile number?**
A: Visit your home branch with your KYC documents, or update it through an ATM using your debit card and PIN. Online updates are not allowed for security reasons. The change takes effect within 24 hours.

**Q: Is there a charge for SMS alerts?**
A: Yes. SMS alerts cost ₹15 + GST per quarter for savings accounts. Email alerts are free. Basic Savings accounts and senior citizens are exempt from the SMS charge.

**Q: Can NRIs open an account?**
A: Yes. NRIs can open NRE and NRO savings accounts and FDs. Interest on NRE accounts is tax-free in India. NRE FD rates are the same as domestic FD rates for tenures of 1 year and above; NRE FDs cannot be opened for less than 1 year.

**Q: How do I get an account statement?**
A: Statements for up to the last 5 years can be downloaded free from the app or NetBanking. A physical statement from the branch costs ₹100 + GST per request. Monthly email statements are free.

**Q: What is the minimum age to open an account on my own?**
A: Minors aged 10 and above can open and operate a Junior Savings account on their own, with limits on withdrawals. Below age 10, the account must be operated by a parent or guardian.
